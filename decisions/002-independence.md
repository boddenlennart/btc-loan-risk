# 002 · Independent and lender-neutral

Date: 2026-10-15, sharpened 2026-10-16 and 2026-10-23 · Status: in force

## Decision
No lender is named, ranked, recommended or paid on the site; no provider presets; examples use made-up round numbers. People from lenders may give feedback; their input is used only to model loan types correctly and never becomes the page's example. How it works will say that practitioners commented and no one paid for or influenced results.

## Why
The owner wants a tool liked by lenders and the community alike, bringing transparency, never hostility to Bitcoin loans. Independence is the moat (lenders' own calculators compare only against themselves).

## Alternatives considered
A lender directory with "no affiliation" disclaimers (postponed, decide after launch with a legal check); naming lenders as examples (rejected).

## Consequences
Wording tests guard advice and market claims; marketing never names lenders either; contacts' names stay out of this public repository.
