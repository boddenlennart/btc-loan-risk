# 007 · Numbers that can be compared agree

Date: 2026-10-21 · Status: in force

## Decision
A number shown is rounded once; numbers next to it are derived from it (for example "zero in X of 100" = 100 − the answer). The futures chart's 100 futures are picked so they contain exactly the answer's number of liquidations. 14 rules are checked on screen for 30 loans, comparisons, How it works and German.

## Why
The owner found pairs that disagreed (13 vs 15 in the chart; 47 + 54 = 101), each correct on its own.

## Alternatives considered
Leaving numbers independent with a note (rejected: costs trust).

## Consequences
New numbers on the page need a rule in e2e/consistency.spec.js.
