What changes, in short
  • One question, default "No": visitors who do not plan to add BTC see no new fields; the suggestion "Keep €… ready for a margin call" (20% of the loan) goes.
  • Four ways, own amounts: each way shows only its own fields (one to four), empty until filled in. Nothing is preset.
  • The answer says what it assumes and links back to the question: "Assumes you add no BTC or money. Will you add some?"
  • Your money, four ways: the visitor's own total, added in each of the other ways, so they see how their plan compares. No way is recommended.
  • For an existing loan the same question appears, counted from today.
Open for the plan
  • The settings of the other ways in table E (here: margin call at the lender's 70%; 65% back to 50% within 1 hour). Possibly taken from the visitor's other entries.
  • Whether the simulation also runs table E (four extra runs) or only real history.
