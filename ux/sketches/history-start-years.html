What changes, in short
  • Findable: the section moves up to second place, right after "What happens when BTC falls". Its title line shows the key numbers while folded. "real history since 2014" under the answer becomes a link that opens it.
  • Title: "Your loan in every crash since 2014" becomes "Your loan in real history" (the table now starts in other years too).
  • Start years: one small table replaces the two sentences: 2014 (all), 2018, 2020, 2022, with start days per row and a short bar. Fixed years, no picker.
  • Kept: the comparison with the answer at the top (shortened), the 90-day table, the four real years chart, the trend question.
  • Rules: the 2014 row equals "real history since 2014" under the answer and the 90-day table's "All start days" row. A year with no start days (very long loans) is left out.
