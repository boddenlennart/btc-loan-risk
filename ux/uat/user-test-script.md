# User test: 20 minutes per person

For 5–8 people from the target group: people who borrow against BTC, plan to, or hold BTC and have thought about it. One person at a time, on their own phone if possible. You watch and take notes; you do not help. If they ask "what should I click?", answer "What would you try?".

**Before:** "This is a test of the page, not of you. Please think aloud: say what you look at, what you expect, what surprises you. Nothing you say is wrong."

**Record:** for each task, done or not, roughly how long, where they hesitated, and their words (exact quotes are gold).

## Tasks

1. **First look (1 min).** Open bitcoinloancheck.com. "What do you think this page does? Who is it for?"
   *Success: they say something like "checks the risk of a Bitcoin loan"; they notice it is not a lender.*
2. **Your own offer (4 min).** "Here is an offer: €20,000, starting LTV 45%, liquidation at 85%, margin call at 75% with 72 hours to top up, 9% a year, 12 months. Check it."
   *Success: all values entered, including the margin call and the 72 hours (these are under "More terms").*
3. **Read the answer (3 min).** "In your own words: what does the big number mean? Would you take this loan?"
   *Do not explain. Note whether they read it as "chance" correctly and whether they find "Liquidated at".*
4. **Make it safer (2 min).** "Make this loan safer. What would you change?"
   *Success: they use "Make it safer" or change the LTV themselves.*
5. **Compare (4 min).** "A second lender offers 7% a year, but liquidates at 80% and has no margin call. Which offer is better for you, and why?"
   *Success: they use "Compare an offer", fill in the second offer, and talk about both risk and cost, not only the rate. Note if they use "Cost including the risk".*
6. **A loan you already have (3 min).** "You took a loan a year ago: you owe €10,000 against 0.4 BTC, 6 months left. Is it safe now? Could you borrow €3,000 more?"
   *Success: they find "A loan I already have" and "Compare with a refinancing offer".*
7. **Trust (1 min).** "Would you trust this number? What would make you trust it more?"

## Comprehension quiz (after the tasks, on paper)

Show the answer of the example loan (54 of 100). Ask:

1. "54 of 100" means… (a) the loan loses 54% of its value · (b) in 54 of 100 simulated futures the loan is liquidated · (c) the lender's risk score is 54. *(b)*
2. "Liquidated at €37,500" means… (a) the price BTC must fall to for the lender to sell · (b) what you get back · (c) the loan amount plus interest. *(a)*
3. "Cost including the risk: about €2,210" means… (a) a fee the lender charges · (b) the interest plus what a liquidation costs you on average · (c) the worst case. *(b)*
4. If BTC rises a lot, the number 54… (a) is already included · (b) would be lower, the page assumes no trend · (c) would be higher. *(b)*
5. Does the number include the risk that the lender goes bankrupt? (yes / no) *(no)*

**Success criterion:** at least 4 of 5 people answer at least 4 of 5 questions correctly, and no question is answered wrongly by more than 1 person. A question many people get wrong points to a text to rewrite.

## After each session (5 min)

- On a scale from 1 (very hard) to 7 (very easy): how easy was it to check an offer?
- What was missing? What would you remove?
- Would you send this page to a friend who wants a Bitcoin loan? Why or why not?
