# 016 · "One change lowers this": the smallest step that clearly helps

Date: 2026-10-21 · Status: in force

## Decision
Under the answer, the suggested change is the smallest starting-LTV step (5, 10 or 20 points lower) that lowers the number by at least a quarter and at least 1 point; only if none does, another kind of change.

## Why
A loan at 25% got "Starting LTV 5% → 0 of 100", which asks for five times the BTC.

## Alternatives considered
The change that lowers most (rejected).

## Consequences
Bigger steps stay visible in "Make it safer".
