# 024 · Words bitcoiners know: price paths, top up, initial LTV

Date: 2026-11-02 · Status: in force

## Decision
The page uses the words of loan services and their users, one word per thing:
- **Simulated price paths**, not "simulated futures" (English). In crypto, "futures" are derivative contracts. German keeps "Zukünfte".
- **Pledge** is the BTC given at the start ("BTC you pledge"). **Top up** is every later addition: at a margin call, in the visitor's plan ("Will you top up if the price falls?"), in "Make it safer" and in "What happens when BTC falls". German keeps "nachschießen".
- **Initial LTV**, not "starting LTV" (English), as lenders write it. German keeps "Start-LTV".
- The time to top up is also named as the **cure period** in its help text, the contract term.
- In "Where to find these numbers", the German contract words are marked as German.

The rules part is titled "If the price falls: your lender's rules and your plan". Its folded line has two groups, "Your lender" and "Your plan", and the open part has the headings "Margin call and sale" and "Your plan".

## Why
The expected visitors already use loan services (owner, 2 Nov 2026). A first-time visitor should find the terms they know, and never two words for the same action (the page said "top up", "add BTC" and "pledge more" for one thing).
