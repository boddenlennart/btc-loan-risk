# 014 · Legal: best practice, no lawyer

Date: 2026-10-18 · Status: in force

## Decision
Impressum (§ 5 DDG, § 18 (2) MStV) with a c/o service address (no home address) and e-mail; privacy notice (GDPR Art. 13) in DE (original) and EN naming every outside service. Accepted risks: the c/o address; e-mail as the only fast contact (to be closed by adding the Online-Impressum contact form, see backlog).

## Why
The owner chose best practice over a lawyer.

## Alternatives considered
Consulting a lawyer (optional, not a blocker).

## Consequences
Privacy notice first for any new service; not financial advice wording everywhere.
