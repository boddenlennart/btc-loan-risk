Logo variants · wordmark + mark, light and dark, and at favicon size

Bitcoin Loan Check

A · Check on the line. A check mark that rests exactly on a line: the sale price, checked. Simple, readable at 16 px. Risk: check marks are common.

Bitcoin Loan Check

B · Distance to the line. The price (dot) above the sale line, with the gap between them. Says "how far until sale". Less obvious at first sight; at 16 px it becomes dot-over-line.

Bitcoin Loan Check

C · Ledger rule. Wordmark with a ruled line ending in a small tick, like a checked line in an account book. Most "print-like"; the small icon needs letters (LC), which read less well at 16 px.
