Phase B · Sketches (not the real page)

All numbers are real, computed with the page's engine for the test offer in A2 (loan €15,000, starting LTV 50%, liquidation 83%, margin call 70% with sale after 48 h, 12 months, 11.5% added to the loan, fee 1.5%) at a BTC price of €72,066. Layout, wording and colours are proposals. Nothing here is interactive except where noted.

1 · Input: five numbers first

Five essential numbers and one question on the first screen. Everything else is behind "More terms from your offer", already filled with starting values. One guide shows where to find the numbers, instead of 23 "where to find" lines.

Input screen (phone)

The results start directly below. There is no "calculate" button: results update as you type.

"Where to find these numbers" (opens on tap)

One small picture of a term sheet with the 5 numbers marked, plus the words to search for. People read their offer once, not field by field.

"More terms from your offer" (open)

The margin-call rules become one plain question with three answers. Here the test offer's "sale after 48 h" is chosen. That is the setting that turned out to matter most.
