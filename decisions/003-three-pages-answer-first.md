# 003 · Three pages, answer first

Date: 2026-10-17 / 2026-10-18 · Status: in force

## Decision
Three pages in English and German: Check a loan (/), Insights (/insights/: what really happened since 2014, never "what is best"), How it works (/method/). The check page is an answer-first report: the answer to the example is visible before any input, terms on the side (desktop) or folded (phone).

## Why
Fragile personas need the answer before the form; method and insights would overload one page.

## Alternatives considered
One long page (rejected: overload); a layout modelled on a lender's site (analysed neutrally, rejected as anchoring).

## Consequences
Content audit forbids repeating content across pages.
