What changes on the check page

  1. Navigation: Check a loan · Insights · How it works.
  2. One hook to the insights at the sale price, so the general data is one tap away exactly where it helps.
  3. "How it works →" in the small print goes to the method page.
  4. Moved away: "How these numbers are made" (to /method); general BTC history facts (to /insights and /method); "Help improve" (footer link).
  5. Repeats removed (8 found): lender failure said 3×, data period 5×, sale price 5×, "your lender measures differently" 2×, "lender's figures win" 2×, privacy summary 2×, method results 3×, history facts 2×.

Effect: the check page loses one section, three general paragraphs and five repeats. Nothing is lost: it moves to the page where people look for it.
